Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first conversation you get a written quote with a fixed price and deadline. If the scope doesn't change, the price stays the same.

How long does it take?

The timeline depends on the scope and is written in the quote. We work in stages and you see a working version after each one, so you can follow the progress the whole time.

We only have an idea, no brief. Is that enough?

Yes. We prepare the brief together during the first conversations and it becomes part of the quote.

Who publishes the app on the App Store and Google Play?

We do. We prepare the descriptions and screenshots, submit the app and work with the Apple and Google review teams until it is approved.

Can you connect the new system to the software we already use?

In most cases, yes. Before the quote we check what connection options each tool offers and tell you what is possible.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Conversations and reviews of the work happen online, and we meet in person when needed.

What happens after launch?

We take over the maintenance: we keep the product running, update it and add new features when you need them. The terms are agreed together with the project.

We don't have good photos for the website. Can you help?

Yes. We work with a professional photographer who shoots your products, premises and team. If you wish, we add the shoot to the quote together with the rest of the work.
